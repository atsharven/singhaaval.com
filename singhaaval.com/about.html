Origin & Spirit

The Spirit of SinghAaval

Rooted in the soil of Singhawal village, Ajmer, we preserve traditional block-printing and hand-embroidery techniques, uplifting families and exporting premium products globally.

Historical Legend

Coming of the Lions

Our village, Singhawal, is nestled near the Aravalli range in Rajasthan. According to local lore, the hills surrounding the ancient Maa Annapurna temple were once the home of magnificent wild lions.

Locals recount how the lions would peacefully descend from the rocks at dusk, bowing their heads before the temple of Maa Annapurna — the goddess of food and preservation. Observing this spiritual harmony, the villagers named their home Singh Aaval, signifying the "Coming of Lions."

We honor this legacy of fierce devotion and noble guardianship. Each bag, cushion cover, and tapestry is a shield of culture, safeguarding historical prints and ensuring fair livelihoods for our artisan collective.

Process & Integrity

Meticulous Slow Production

Our workshop uses teak wood blocks carved by master carvers. Artisans press each block by hand onto premium unbleached cotton. This process demands absolute patience — a single yard of fabric can require over a hundred precise block strikes.

For our patchworks and wall tapestries, we collect vintage textile pieces, cataloging them by color, condition, and origin. Local women artisans arrange and hand-stitch these components into geometric patterns, giving old heritage cotton a second life.

This deliberate handwork causes beautiful variations. These tiny print offsets and needle variations are not mistakes; they are the fingerprints of human artistry.

Our Tenets

The Export Promise

Quality Assurance

Every shipment passes double quality checkpoints. We check weight, seams, block alignment, and color bleeding resistance, matching premium global retail criteria.

Fair Trade Practices

We support fair wages, safe community workspaces, and flexible hours for mothers. By dealing directly, we guarantee profits return directly to Ajmer's craftspeople.

Smooth Logistics

We coordinate all export declarations, proper HS classification, and standard commercial packing. We partner with reliable air and ocean freights for hassle-free custom clearances.

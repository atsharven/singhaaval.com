Our Narrative

The Legend of SinghAaval

Singhawal is a quiet village in the Ajmer district of Rajasthan. Resting atop a small hill lies the sacred temple of Maa Annapurna. Legend tells that lions once roamed freely in these hills, descending each evening to bow before the deity. From this reverence arose the name Singhawal — Singh Aaval, the coming of lions.

"We represent the fearless guardians of Rajasthani craftsmanship, weaving stories into block prints."

Every piece is handcrafted in small, ethical batches — block printed tote bags, duffle bags, vanity pouches, patchwork wall hangings, and heritage bolster covers.

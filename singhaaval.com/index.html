Our Narrative

The Legend of SinghAaval

Singhawal is a quiet village in the Ajmer district of Rajasthan. Resting atop a small hill lies the sacred temple of Maa Annapurna, the Goddess of Food and Nourishment. Legend tells that lions once roamed freely in these hills, and each evening they would descend to bow before the deity. From this reverence arose the name Singhawal — Singh Aaval, meaning the coming of lions.

"We represent the fearless guardians of Rajasthani craftsmanship, weaving stories into block prints."

Every piece in our collection is handcrafted in small, ethical batches. We work closely with local artisans to produce beautiful block printed tote bags, duffle bags, vanity pouches, patchwork wall hangings, and heritage bolster covers, celebrating the natural variations that make handmade items precious.

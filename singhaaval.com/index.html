The Legend of SinghAaval

Our Story - Singhawal is a tiny village in the Ajmer district of Rajasthan. Nestled on a small hill lies the sacred temple of Maa Annapurna —Goddess of Food & Nourishment. Local legend tells that lions once roamed freely in the area, and each night they would bow before Maa Annapurna in reverence. From this divine connection came the name Singhawal — Singh Aaval, meaning "Coming of Lions."

The name "Singh Aaval" translates to the "Coming of Lions," representing the fearless guardians of our heritage. We carry forward this fierce dedication, weaving the village's rich narratives into every block print, every stitch, and every molded clay.

# Elephant Wall Hanging

Square decorative tapestry featuring a handblock printed royal elephant in classic Rajasthan court style, flanked by floral block stamps. Natural indigo dye on hand-loomed cotton.

## Craftsmanship

This piece celebrates Rajasthan's legendary block printing tradition. Handblock printed by artisans using natural indigo dye and traditional techniques passed down through generations.

## Specifications

- **Material:** Loomed Cotton with Indigo Dye
- **Dimensions:** 90 × 90 cm
- **HS Code:** HS 63049210
- **Packaging:** 20 units per moisture-sealed bale

## Design Features

- Royal elephant motif in classic Rajasthan court style
- Floral block stamps flanking the elephant
- Natural indigo dye (hand-processed)
- Hand-loomed cotton base
- Traditional block printing technique
- Square format for versatile placement

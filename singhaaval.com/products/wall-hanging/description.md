# Patchwork Wall Hanging

Gorgeous wall tapestry assembled from wedding saree parts, heavy beadwork, and layered stitch backings. Complete hanging loops integrated. Each piece is a unique collage.

## Craftsmanship

This spectacular wall piece celebrates the artistry of upcycling. Made from vintage saree fragments, wedding textiles, and careful hand-stitching, each hanging is truly one-of-a-kind.

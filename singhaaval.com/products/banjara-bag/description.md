# Embroidered Banjara Bag

Exquisite shoulder bag with traditional Banjara mirrorwork, metallic coin appliqués, and hand-stitched borders. Features zip closure and interior compartments.

## Craftsmanship

Each piece takes 3–4 days of dedicated artisan work. Hand-embroidered with vintage textile patches, mirror work sourced from local Ajmer craftspeople, and eco-friendly, chemical-free dyes.

## Specifications

- **Material:** Organic Handspun Cotton Canvas
- **Dimensions:** 42 × 35 × 12 cm
- **HS Code:** HS 42022220
- **Packaging:** 50 units per heavy-duty carton

## Perfect For

- Luxury retail boutiques
- European export markets
- Corporate gifting
- Collectors of heritage textiles

## Care Instructions

- Hand wash recommended
- Avoid direct sunlight
- Store in cool, dry place
- Air dry completely before storage
